Fly to Anchorage

For those coming from outside Alaska, Ted Stevens Anchorage International Airport (ANC) is the closest major airport.

Get to Seward

Seward is about 125 miles south of Anchorage. Drive the very scenic Seward Highway roughly 2.5 hours, or take the Alaska Railroad’s Coastal Classic train.

What to pack

The wedding will be casual with a side of special occasion, just wear what makes you comfortable to celebrate. June in Alaska is usually mild but can change quickly — bring layers... a rain jacket, warm mid-layers, and comfortable shoes for hiking. Expect nearly 24 hours of light, so eye masks for sleeping are recommended for those unfamiliar with northern summer.

For the adventurous, there is sea kayaking right from the lodge, fishing opportunities from the beach, swimming, hiking up the ridge, and even snorkeling along the rocky coast, swell permitting.
